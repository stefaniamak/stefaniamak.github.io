About

Hey, I'm Stefania Makrygiannaki, a Senior Flutter Software Engineer from Thessaloniki, Greece.

I've been building mobile apps since 2020 and have worked on over 20 production applications, reaching more than 1 million downloads worldwide.

Over the years, I've led teams, mentored developers, and collaborated closely with designers, product teams, and clients to turn ideas into reliable, well-crafted products. I care deeply about clarity, quality, and building things that feel as good to use as they are to maintain.

Outside of coding, you'll often find me doing photography, cosplaying fictional characters, or writing about development. Always curious, always painting.
